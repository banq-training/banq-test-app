# Your Website

This folder holds your website. On Monday you ask Copilot to build your page with
`/create-website`.

- **index.html** will be your page.
- Your own ideas get their own folders under **ideas/**, so your first page is never replaced.
- `/start-website` opens your page beside Chat, and `/stop-website` closes it. Your files stay.
- Only put things here you would happily show on a big screen: made-up content, no real names,
  customers, patients, or work records, and no private files.
- On Tuesday you write your **Next** wishes at the end of this file, and on Wednesday your
  requirements and how to run your website.

Your page remembers the entries you add in this browser only; it is not a shared database.
Publishing is optional. Start with [your first website](../docs/DAY-1.md#3-your-first-website).
